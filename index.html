Winner Rules

The visible guardrails used by Product Hunter
💰 EconomicsTarget selling price $50–$80, minimum $40, COGS up to ~$35 and minimum 3× markup.
⭐ Proof of demandMinimum rating 4.0 and 50+ reviews; successful existing sellers are a positive signal.
🎯 Customer clarityPrioritize products with a clear golden customer and an obvious pain point or passion point.
🔥 Wow factorPrefer products with strong perceived value and differentiation rather than commodity items.
📦 LogisticsSmall, lightweight and easy to ship. Reject electronics, fragile/glass and ingestible products.
🌱 EvergreenAvoid seasonal products. Prefer products with frequent or repeat use.
🎥 Content potentialPrioritize products you can realistically demonstrate and make compelling content around.
🏆 Score bands0–59 Reject · 60–69 Watch · 70–79 Test · 80–89 Strong Winner · 90–100 Exceptional.
Important: these are the product-selection rules exposed in the UI. The backend remains the source of truth for actual scoring.
